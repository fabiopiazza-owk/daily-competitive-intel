## 6. Build Signals for K Platform

### 🥇 Proteomic + multi-modal aging/disease clock module
**Rationale:** The Nature Biotech Phase 2a paper proves regulators/sponsors accept clock-based endpoints in interventional trials. K's multimodal advantage (pathology + genomics + clinical) means we can ship a differentiated "clock stack" that beats single-omic vendors, and it slots directly into ongoing AstraZeneca/Sanofi biomarker conversations.
**Effort:** Medium — 1 quarter for MVP wrapping existing proteomic clocks + 1 histology-derived clock; requires validation partnership with a geroscience sponsor.

**Rationale:** Reconstructing signaling histories from limited in vivo samples using pre-trained perturbation embeddings is a capability Biomni will race to. Owning this early strengthens K's "mechanism-of-action explainability" story, which is a key GxP + payer conversation.
**Effort:** Medium-high — 1-2 quarters; needs licensed access to a large perturbation atlas (Tahoe/JUMP-CP) and a foundation-model retraining budget.

**Rationale:** Contrastive AE representations (PubMed paper) + growing payer/FDA scrutiny (Rasonque access story) create demand for interpretable safety-signal tooling. Bolt-on to K's clinical trial optimization module; unlocks post-approval RWE upsell.
**Effort:** Low-medium — 6-8 weeks for a v1 leveraging FAERS + client trial safety data; requires clinical-safety SME review.
