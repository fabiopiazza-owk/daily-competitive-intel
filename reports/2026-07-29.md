## 6. Build Signals for K Platform

### 🥇 Clinical Readiness Claims Dossier Generator
**Rationale:** Two independent signals today (PubMed governance paper + STAT benchmarking critique) show that pharma and regulators are moving from "what's your AUC?" to "what claims does that AUC support?" K should ship a Claims Substantiation Module that ties every model output to (a) intended use, (b) validation cohort, (c) regulatory context (FDA/EMA/EU AI Act high-risk classification). This becomes a moat vs. Biomni and OpenAI agents which have no such dossier layer.
**Effort:** M — 1 quarter, mostly product + regulatory affairs collaboration; leverages existing model cards.

### 🥈 Pathology + Spatial Proteomics Integrated Workflow
**Rationale:** The Nature Methods proteomics Perspective and the melanoma spatial-proteomics case report converge on the same near-term productization: multimodal pathology + spatial-omics in one biomarker workflow. K owns the pathology foundation model; adding a spatial-proteomics adapter (Akoya/CosMx/Visium ingestion + co-registration) closes a differentiator before Benchling wraps this via partnerships.
**Effort:** L — 2 quarters; requires new ingestion connectors, alignment pipeline, and one flagship pharma co-development.

### 🥉 Agentic Memory Layer for Long-Horizon Discovery Workflows
**Rationale:** UniMem and the multi-teacher long-horizon planning paper both point to a maturing pattern: agents that persist across weeks/months of a discovery campaign. K's current agent surface is largely single-session. A memory layer (episodic + parametric) with GxP-compatible audit trails would let AstraZeneca/Sanofi teams run continuous target-ID or biomarker-discovery agents — and preempts Biomni's likely next move.
**Effort:** M/L — 1.5 quarters; requires memory store, retrieval eval harness, and audit logging aligned to 21 CFR Part 11.
