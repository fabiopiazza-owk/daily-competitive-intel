## 6. Build Signals for K Platform

### 🥇 Calibration-first evaluation harness for all K predictive modules
**Rationale:** The Nature Biotech perturbation paper reframes the entire evaluation debate around calibration. Pharma scientific reviewers will now ask for calibration curves and ECE, not just AUC/AUPRC. Shipping this first makes K the "trustworthy" option vs. Gemini 4 Argon's raw-capability pitch.
**Effort:** Medium (2–3 sprints) — add calibration metrics + reliability diagrams to the existing eval pipeline, backfill model cards.

### 🥈 Provenance & watermarking layer for generative biology outputs
**Rationale:** SynthID Bio signals a near-term regulatory expectation for traceable AI-generated molecules/proteins. Being able to tell AstraZeneca/Sanofi "every K-generated candidate carries a verifiable provenance tag auditable for FDA submissions" is a differentiator before competitors catch up.
**Effort:** Large (1 quarter) — requires crypto-signing of generation runs, metadata schema, integration with model registry.

### 🥉 OpenTumorBoard-style eval for K's clinical trial / MDT module
**Rationale:** A public, multimodal, multi-turn benchmark on real tumor board trajectories is the exact external yardstick enterprise buyers will cite. Running K against it (and publishing) converts a research signal into sales ammunition and roadmap feedback simultaneously.
**Effort:** Small-to-medium (3–4 weeks) — benchmark adaptation, internal scorecard, publishable blog post.
