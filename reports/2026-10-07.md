## 6. Build Signals for K Platform

### 🥇 Ship Conformal-Prediction uncertainty wrappers for all biomarker & stratification outputs
**Rationale:** New theoretical grounding ([arxiv 2610.08785](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08785v1)) + FDA/Utah regulatory ambiguity means K must differentiate on *calibrated, auditable uncertainty* — not just raw predictions. This is a defensible wedge vs. OpenAI/DeepMind's general models and a procurement unlock for AZ/Sanofi regulatory teams.
**Effort:** 1 sprint for pilot module on one indication; 1 quarter for platform-wide rollout with GxP documentation.

### 🥈 "Intelligent UI" upgrade for K Research Copilot
**Rationale:** GPT-6's interactive-visual default resets user expectations overnight. Pharma scientists exploring Kaplan-Meier curves, pathway maps, or cohort selections in K will compare directly to ChatGPT. Static-plot UX is now a liability.
**Effort:** 2 sprints for interactive viz framework + 3 flagship workflows (cohort builder, biomarker explorer, trial simulator).

**Rationale:** The AD paper ([PubMed 42840584](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42840584/)) + IdeaAnchor ([arxiv 2610.08781](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08781v1)) converge on the same pattern Biomni is chasing. K's defensible edge is multimodal priors (pathology + omics) grounding the ideation loop — ship a target-discovery agent that cites and ranks hypotheses against K's internal foundation models.
**Effort:** 1 quarter for v1 scoped to oncology targets; needs eval harness co-designed with an AZ or Sanofi target-ID team.
