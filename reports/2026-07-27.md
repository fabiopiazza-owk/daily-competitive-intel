## 3. Technical Frontier — top papers relevant to K capabilities
- **[Agentic Context Management](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.21503)** — treats agent memory/cost as lifecycle+architecture problems. Directly applicable to K agents handling long pathology reports, trial protocols, and multi-omics tool outputs.
- **[The Regression Tax: Why Skills Help and Hurt LLM Agents](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22520v1)** — 6,000-run study shows procedural skills can degrade agents. Critical for K: adding "pathology skill," "genomics skill," etc. is not free — we need per-skill regression testing.
- **[Multi-Head Latent Control for LLM Agents](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.14277)** — unified interface for deciding to defer, escalate, request info. Perfect fit for K's clinical-trial and biomarker agents that must know when to escalate to human experts (GxP requirement).
- **[IDEAgent: Quality-Diversity Search for Research Ideas](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.22375)** — drug discovery hypothesis generation analog; competitive pressure signal for Biomni-like features.
- **[Scaling Native Multimodal Pre-Training](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.22043)** — reinforces that pathology+genomics+clinical joint pre-training (not late fusion) is where the field is heading; relevant to K's foundation model strategy.
