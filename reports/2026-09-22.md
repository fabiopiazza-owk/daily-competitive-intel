## 6. Build Signals for K Platform

### 🥇 GPT-6 Sol/Luna evaluation harness + routing policy
**Rationale:** GPT-6 launched today with an explicit cost/capability split. Every pharma buyer will ask "are you on the latest?" within the week. We need benchmark numbers on pathology-VQA, clinical reasoning, and regulatory QA, plus a cost-aware routing policy (Luna for RAG rerank/summarization, Sol for reasoning-heavy steps).

**Rationale:** Sino-US-DrugQA is a public benchmark our AstraZeneca/Sanofi teams will care about. Scoring well — and publishing an extended EMA-inclusive version — turns a compliance story into a marketing asset and differentiates against Benchling and generic LLM plays.
**Effort:** 3 weeks — evals + regulatory SME review.

### 🥉 D-RAC-style multimodal ingestion upgrade for pharma documents
**Rationale:** Our current PDF/CSR/SOP ingestion loses table structure and multi-column layout — a recurring complaint from Sanofi ops. D-RAC's PDF-normalization + multimodal markdown pipeline is a well-scoped upgrade with immediate retrieval-quality wins, and pairs naturally with the FinOps/KV-cache attribution work as a "trusted enterprise ingestion" bundle.
**Effort:** 4-6 weeks — 1 backend + 1 ML eng, phased rollout.
