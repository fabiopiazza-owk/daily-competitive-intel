## 6. Build Signals for K Platform

### 🥇 Ship a "Universal Pharma Document Ingestion" service based on D-RAC principles
**Rationale:** Every K enterprise deal stalls on document heterogeneity (protocols in Word, CSRs in PDF, pathology reports as scans, tables everywhere). D-RAC's PDF normalization + multimodal markdown conversion is the right abstraction and it's now public. Owning this layer eliminates per-customer ingestion consulting, accelerates deal ramp, and creates a defensible retrieval quality floor that generalist stacks (V7, OpenAI) can't match without pharma-schema knowledge.
**Effort:** Large (1 quarter, 2–3 engineers + 1 ML) — reference-implement D-RAC, add pharma-specific schemas (CDISC, HL7, DICOM sidecars), evaluate against internal AZ/Sanofi corpus.

### 🥈 Establish K's regulatory-QA benchmark position (Sino-US-DrugQA + internal EU AI Act eval set)
**Rationale:** Pharma procurement is 6 months from asking "what's your score on Sino-US-DrugQA?" We should run the eval now, publish results, and extend with an EU AI Act / EMA companion set that becomes our benchmark. First-mover on the eval narrative sets the terms of comparison against Biomni and generalist LLMs.
**Effort:** Medium (4–6 weeks, 1 ML + 1 regulatory SME) — run public benchmark, author whitepaper, extend eval set.

### 🥉 Prototype system-1-controlled agentic memory (Jev-Mem-style) for K's clinical agents
**Rationale:** K's long-horizon agents (trial optimization, biomarker exploration) are increasingly memory-bound and latency-bound. Taking the LLM off the critical path of memory ops is a durable cost + UX win, and positions us ahead of V7's agent-memory pitch. Also derisks per-token cost exposure as usage scales in enterprise deployments.
**Effort:** Medium (6–8 weeks spike, 2 engineers) — replicate Jev-Mem on one K agent workflow, measure latency/cost delta, decide on productionization.
