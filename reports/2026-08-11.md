## 6. Build Signals for K Platform

### 🥇 GxP-ready Pharmacovigilance / ADE module with LLM+RAG
**Rationale:** Peer-reviewed ADE frameworks are now public, competitors will productize within 2 quarters, and existing K customers (AZ, Sanofi) already have unmet PV needs. Bundling ADE detection with K's clinical data ingestion creates a low-friction expansion sale and demonstrates regulated-workflow chops for future FDA-facing claims.
**Effort:** Medium (8–12 weeks) — leverage existing K data connectors + RAG stack; the hard part is GxP validation documentation and FAERS integration.

### 🥈 Agentic drug-discovery workflow layer with auditable trajectories
**Rationale:** Biomni and the "Eroom's Law" agentic-AI thesis are converging into a marketable category. K needs a defensible agent layer that orchestrates target ID → hit finding → biomarker discovery with full audit trails (leveraging the [Agent Auditing Engine](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.07346) pattern). Without this, K risks being framed as a "models + dashboards" vendor while competitors own the "autonomous R&D copilot" narrative.
**Effort:** Large (2 quarters) — requires agent orchestration, tool registry, audit log store, and 2–3 flagship end-to-end workflows with pharma design partners.

### 🥉 Multimodal interpretability & feature-diffing dashboard for pathology+genomics models
**Rationale:** EU AI Act high-risk obligations plus the FDA's rising evidentiary bar (see [AI Echo RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42571809/)) mean K must ship explainability that regulators and pharma safety committees accept. [Multimodal Model Diffing](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09928v1) offers a research-grade blueprint; productizing it as a customer-facing "why did the model decide this" view is a differentiator vs. NVIDIA Clara / Benchling.
**Effort:** Medium (10–14 weeks) — build on existing SAE research, wrap in a per-case UI, integrate into K's pathology viewer.
