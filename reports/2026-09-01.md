## 6. Build Signals for K Platform

### 🥇 Adaptive active-learning virtual screening agent
**Rationale:** Nature Biotech validates the paradigm at library scale; Biomni will move here first. K needs a first-class discovery agent that orchestrates docking + ML surrogate models with human-in-loop rubric evaluation. Direct AZ/Sanofi discovery-team pull.
**Effort:** Large (1–2 quarters, needs compute infra + validated benchmarks)

### 🥈 Critique-aware guardrails for long-horizon K agents (CAST-style)
**Rationale:** As K agents touch EDC, LIMS, and regulatory submissions, single-wrong-action risk is existential. CAST pattern intercepts irreversible tool calls before execution — becomes core to GxP/HIPAA compliance story and audit readiness.
**Effort:** Medium (6–8 weeks; add critique layer to existing agent runtime)

### 🥉 Multiomics reference-calibrator QC in ingestion pipeline
**Rationale:** Nature Biotech signals this will become a regulatory expectation for AI biomarker submissions. Being first to bake calibrator-aware QC into pathology + genomics ingestion is a defensible procurement differentiator and pre-empts FDA questions.
**Effort:** Small–Medium (4–6 weeks; extend existing QC modules with calibrator schema + reporting)
