**Rationale:** PathScaleBench establishes a public yardstick for magnification-shift behavior — the exact failure mode pharma pathology leads probe in due diligence. Being first to publish strong PathScaleBench numbers (and build an internal regression gate) converts a technical risk into a sales asset for AstraZeneca/Sanofi pathology-AI deals.

### 🥈 Adaptive evidence-routing RAG ("K-RECAST") for multimodal pharma corpora
**Rationale:** Fixed-similarity RAG is the single biggest complaint from K design-partner scientists querying heterogeneous sources (pathology reports, trial protocols, omics annotations, literature). RECAST-style adaptive routing + MCP-exposed tool use would materially improve answer quality and position K ahead of Biomni's agent demos.
**Effort:** L (one quarter: routing policy, evaluator set, MCP tool wiring, scientist UAT).

### 🥉 Conditional-memory knowledge updates (EngramEdit-style) for continuously refreshed trial/biomarker knowledge
**Rationale:** Pharma clients demand that K's agents reflect last-week's readouts and FDA actions without retraining or hallucination. EngramEdit's decoupled knowledge path lets us ship weekly knowledge updates with auditability — a direct GxP/validation win and ongoing-ARR hook.
**Effort:** L (prototype on one domain — oncology trial outcomes — then generalize; 8–10 weeks for v1).
