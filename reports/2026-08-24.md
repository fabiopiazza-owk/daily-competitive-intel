## 6. Build Signals for K Platform

### 🥇 Run K's multimodal pathology/lab stack against VIALS and publish results
**Rationale:** VIALS is the first credible external benchmark covering exactly the artifact classes (gel blots, microscopy, flow cytometry, molecular structures) that K's differentiators depend on. Publishing scores establishes K's technical leadership before Biomni or Clara claim the space. If we underperform, we get a diagnostic gap-list for next quarter's model work.
**Effort:** ~2 eng-weeks (eval harness + report). Blog post + arxiv submission adds 1 week.

### 🥈 Prototype treatment-conditioned latent-transition module (PerturbRx-style) in the biomarker service
**Rationale:** AstraZeneca and Sanofi conversations increasingly ask for *predicted* response under specific regimens, not just prognostic biomarkers. Building on PerturbRx's formulation lets us pitch a mechanism-aware response predictor, defensible against Benchling's ELN-adjacent analytics.
**Effort:** ~1 quarter for a scoped oncology proof-of-concept on internal TCGA + partner cohorts; requires 1 ML scientist + 1 clinical data engineer.

### 🥉 Ship "K Governance Pack" — risk-tiered AI-use policy templates for pharma + hospital review boards
**Rationale:** Fierce Healthcare + AMA debates show enterprise buyers are actively rewriting AI review criteria. A ready-made, risk-tiered governance pack (narrow AI vs. generative, PHI envelope, GxP validation checklists, EU AI Act mapping) shortens procurement cycles and de-risks the Sanofi and next-tier pharma expansions.
**Effort:** ~3 weeks — regulatory lead + solutions engineering + legal review. High leverage per hour.
